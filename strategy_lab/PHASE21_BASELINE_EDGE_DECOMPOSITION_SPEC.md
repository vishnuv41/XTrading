## 1. Forensic Dissection Dimensions

Phase 21 performs 8 exhaustive diagnostic evaluations:

1. **Asset-Level Attribution**:
   - Individual asset Sharpe, return, profit factor, win rate, expectancy, and contribution to total return/drawdown.
2. **Year-by-Year Calendar Attribution**:
   - Performance breakdown across distinct market cycles (2020 bull, 2021 double-top, 2022 bear, 2023 recovery, 2024–2026 holdout).
3. **Market Regime Decomposition**:
   - Causal state breakdown (Trending Bull, Trending Bear, Choppy/Sideways, High Volatility, Low Volatility).
4. **Trade-Level Expectancy Distribution**:
   - Individual closed trade distribution (median trade, average win/loss, payoff ratio, profit factor, skewness).
5. **Tail Dependence & Profit Concentration**:
   - Concentration analysis: % of net cumulative profit generated by Top 5 and Top 10% of trades.
6. **Exposure & Cash Mechanics**:
   - Average market exposure, time in cash, exposure during winning vs losing regimes.
7. **Friction Sensitivity & Break-Even Curve**:
   - Multi-point sweep from 0 to 100 bps round-trip friction to find the exact economic break-even threshold.
8. **Descriptive Benchmark Landscape**:
   - Paired comparison against `Buy & Hold`, `Static 45% Cash`, `Trend_EMA_20`, and `Trend_EMA_100`.
